# Panchayati Raj branding

The signed-out staff, BPRO and DPRO pages show Panchayati Raj branding. Signed-in pages retain the Araria Staff Attendance header and the staff member's assigned department name. All three login routes use the same Bihar emblem and an original illustration of a panchayat office; the illustration is decorative and contains no personal data.

The emblem is the unmodified [Bihar emblem SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e9/Seal_of_Bihar.svg), served locally from `public/bihar-emblem.svg` so login does not depend on Wikimedia availability. The official [Bihar State Electronics Development Corporation website](https://bsedc.bihar.gov.in/en/) identifies it as the Bihar government's emblem. Neither image URL contains staff information.
